# Factory follow-on updates

## Worker retention and optional cleanup

Keep Factory worker threads and workspaces after completion or merge. Do not automatically archive either. Owners must be able to reopen the original conversation, read its final response and inspect its checklist and changes.

Configurable worker auto-archive is deferred until after the operating MVP and disabled by default. An owner could opt into a window such as 24 hours after workspace creation. Only workspaces identified as Factory workers would qualify, and cleanup must wait for terminal work, completed publication and settled executions. Retain conversation history and provide a way to reopen it. Durable Factory and Builds coordinators are excluded.
